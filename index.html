The heart behind the space

Hi, I’m Rinshidha. Let’s take this one step at a time.

As a Consultant Psychologist (MSc Psychology), I believe healing doesn’t require you to be "fixed" — because you are not broken. It begins when you finally feel truly seen, accepted, and heard without judgment.

In our sessions, there is no ticking clock of expectations. We look at the root of what hurts, uncover your innate resilience, and weave gentle, practical psychological tools into your everyday life.
