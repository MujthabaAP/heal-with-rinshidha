## Brand & Style

The design system projects quiet prestige, grounded ecological consciousness, and organic refinement. Drawing inspiration from high-end landscape architecture, sustainable luxury lifestyle, and biophilic editorial design, it balances deep evergreen presence with soft, natural sage neutrals. 

The aesthetic style is **Organic Minimalism** meets **Refined Contemporary Modernism**. It prioritizes generous negative space, immaculate typographic hierarchies, delicate low-contrast structural dividers, and deliberate tonal layering. Interfaces feel calm, grounded, and authoritative without appearing cold or sterile.

## Colors

The color system is derived directly from an organic botanical scheme:

- **Primary (`#224229`)**: A deep, dignified forest green acting as the core anchor for hero CTAs, structural brand moments, and primary accents.
- **Secondary (`#E3EBE0`)**: A pale, soft sage tone used for elevated card containers, chip backgrounds, soft outlines, and secondary interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#1A1D1A`)**: A soft mineral charcoal providing deep contrast for typographic elements while avoiding the harshness of pure black.
- **Canvas Base (`#F6F8F4`)**: The warm off-white, light herbal tint that establishes the default page background and provides a soft, non-glare foundation.

Use high-contrast pairings: deep forest green text or charcoal over secondary sage surfaces, and clean light canvas backgrounds across broad viewport areas.

## Typography

The typography relies entirely on **Manrope**, leveraging its geometric purity and open counters to project calm precision. 

Headings feature tight letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and balanced semi-bold weights (`600`) to create a cohesive editorial presence. Body text preserves spacious line heights (`1.5` to `1.65`) for effortless long-form reading. Micro-labels and tags employ slightly tracked, uppercase or medium-weight typography for clean legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system utilizes a responsive 12-column fluid grid system on desktop that gracefully collapses to an 8-column grid on tablet and a 4-column grid on mobile:
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12 columns, max-width `1280px`, `gutter` of `1.5rem`, outer `margin` of `3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `gutter` of `1.25rem`, outer `margin` of `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter` of `1rem`, outer `margin` of `1.25rem`.

The rhythm is generous and breathing. Ample vertical margins between sections (`3rem` to `5rem`) allow each content block to hold space independently without claustrophobia.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is primarily communicated through **tonal layering** and **low-contrast borders**, steering away from heavy, artificial drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Background surface in `#F6F8F4`.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Flat surfaces in `#E3EBE0` or pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle 1px border using `#224229` at 10% opacity.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Overlays & Popovers)**: Pure white background with an ultra-soft ambient shadow (`0 8px 32px rgba(26, 29, 26, 0.06)`) and a delicate outline (`1px solid rgba(34, 66, 41, 0.08)`).
- **Interactive Hover**: Surfaces lift smoothly using a gentle translate-y (`-2px`) accompanied by a soft tinted bloom (`0 12px 24px rgba(34, 66, 41, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a balanced, tailored corner radius (`roundedness: 2`). Base interactive controls (buttons, inputs) utilize an `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius, while cards and floating modular containers scale to `16px` (`1rem`). Soft badges and tags may selectively adopt full pill geometry to provide organic contrast against structural grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid deep forest green (`#224229`) background with canvas-tinted text (`#F6F8F4`). Height: 44px (md), 52px (lg). Hover brings a slight deepening of tone with a 1px vertical translation.
- **Secondary**: Tinted sage container (`#E3EBE0`) with `#224229` text. Hover transitions background toward pure white.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent background with `#224229` text and a subtle 1px border at 20% opacity.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped or 8px rounded capsules. Secondary sage background (`#E3EBE0`) paired with `#224229` body-sm/label-sm typography. Active chips invert to solid `#224229` with `#F6F8F4` label.

### Form Inputs
- Built on white or light surface backgrounds with an inset feel: 1px border in `#224229` at 15% opacity, rounding of 8px, and 12px horizontal padding. Focus state switches to a solid 1.5px `#224229` border with a subtle 3px spread ring at 8% opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 20px controls with a 1.5px border in `#224229`. Checked states fill solidly with `#224229` and present crisp `#F6F8F4` checkmark icons.

### Cards
- Surfaces rendered in `#E3EBE0` or white, bordered with a faint 1px divider (`rgba(34, 66, 41, 0.08)`). Internal content padding conforms strictly to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), separating headline typography and media effortlessly.

### Lists & Dividers
- Unobtrusive horizontal dividers built with `1px solid rgba(26, 29, 26, 0.08)`. List items maintain generous vertical tap targets (minimum 48px) with subtle hover row highlighting.
